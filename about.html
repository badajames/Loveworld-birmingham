Our Leadership

Meet Our Pastor

Pastor Osondu Atuonwu

Pastor Osondu Atuonwu is a dynamic teacher of the word of God, passionate about bringing men and women into their inheritance in Christ. With a unique grace for ministry and a heart for people, he leads Loveworld Birmingham with a vision to see lives transformed by the power of the Holy Spirit.

Under his leadership, Loveworld Birmingham has become a place of worship, miracles, and purpose, impacting the city of Birmingham and beyond with the gospel of Jesus Christ.

Our Vision & Mission
