Christ Embassy Birmingham

Welcome to Christ Embassy, also known as Loveworld. We are a global ministry with a vision to take God's divine presence to the nations of the world.

Our branch here in Birmingham is led by Pastor Osondu Atuonwu, a place where you can learn more about God's word, experience His love, and witness the improved quality of your life. We invite you to join us for our services and be blessed.
