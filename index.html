Welcome

Welcome to Loveworld Birmingham

Loveworld Birmingham is more than a church. When you worship with us, you learn more than just the letters; it's the spirit of the word. The Spirit of God gets a hold of your life, and then His vision becomes real, not only to you, but He makes that vision real in such a way that others get to know what the word of God really is by watching the word of God manifested in your life every day.

Our branch here in Birmingham is led by Pastor Osondu Atuonwu, a dynamic teacher of God's word with a passion for bringing men and women into their inheritance in Christ. Join us and experience a life-transforming encounter with God's presence.
